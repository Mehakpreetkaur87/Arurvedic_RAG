================================================================================
TROUBLESHOOTING
================================================================================

Issue: Pipeline not initialized
  Solution: python scripts/initialize.py

Issue: GEMINI_API_KEY invalid
  Solution: Get key from https://makersuite.google.com/app/apikey

Issue: Ollama connection refused
  Solution: Start Ollama (ollama serve) in another terminal

Issue: Out of memory
  Solution: Reduce CHUNK_SIZE to 400 in .env

Issue: Slow responses
  Solution: Use Gemini instead of Ollama, or lower MAX_TOKENS

See README.md Troubleshooting section for more solutions
